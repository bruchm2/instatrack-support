Frequently asked questions

Which tracks are supported?

Tor Poznań is the first supported circuit. Support for additional tracks in Poland and internationally is planned for future updates.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. instaTRACK is designed to work offline, and session data is stored locally on your device.

How does OBD-II telemetry connect?

OBD-II telemetry uses compatible Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices. Compatibility can vary by adapter and vehicle.

Where are onboard videos saved?

Finished onboard videos are saved to your iPhone Photos library when you choose to save them.

How can I report a bug or suggest a track?

Email maciejbruchit@gmail.com with a short description. Suggestions for new tracks are welcome.
